# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers who land on the portfolio and need to find the key signal (stack, projects, work rights) in under 30 seconds. Secondary: technical engineers who read deeper and appreciate craft. The primary audience skims; the secondary audience rewards detail.

## Product Purpose

A personal developer portfolio for Poojan Manojkumar Patel. Its job is to make a recruiter immediately understand: (1) what he builds, (2) that he ships real production work, (3) that he has full Australian work rights. Success looks like a recruiter bookmarking it and an engineer respecting the craft on first scroll.

## Brand Personality

System-awakened, conviction, edge. The aesthetic draws from the Shadow Slave web novel: a System HUD crossed with nightmare corruption. Dark void backgrounds, ice-blue primary accent, nightmare purple for emphasis, legendary gold used sparingly. This is not a generic dev portfolio - it is a committed creative identity.

## Anti-references

- Generic light-mode portfolio with a white background and pastel accents
- SaaS-cream / warm-neutral "minimal" portfolios
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + body
- Gradient text on hero headings
- Glassmorphism used decoratively
- Numbered section markers (01 / 02 / 03) as scaffolding
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section

## Design Principles

1. **Identity over safety** - the Shadow Slave aesthetic is the brand. Do not soften it for broad appeal; it is the differentiator.
2. **Signal before story** - recruiters skim. Stack, role, and work rights must be findable in under 10 seconds without reading body copy.
3. **Earn every element** - the HUD and corruption effects exist because they serve the narrative. Decoration that does not carry meaning gets cut.
4. **Production proof over claims** - show real metrics, live URLs, and CI/CD pipelines. Adjectives like "strong" are replaced by specifics.
5. **Responsive without compromise** - the void aesthetic must hold on mobile; effects that degrade on small screens get rebuilt, not hidden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline. Body text (#c8c0e0 on #030309) must maintain 4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion alternative required for all animations (scanlines, particle pulses, entrance reveals). Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. No content gated behind animation completion.
